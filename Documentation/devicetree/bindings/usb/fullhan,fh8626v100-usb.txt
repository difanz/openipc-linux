Fullhan FH8626V100 USB host
==========================

Synopsys DWC2 with integrated UTMI PHY and Fullhan PMU sequence.
Required properties:
- compatible: "fullhan,fh8626v100-usb" (no generic fallback)
- reg: controller window, 0xe0700000 / 0x4000 on V100
- interrupts: hardware source 27
- clocks: USB functional clock and USB HCLK gate
- clock-names: "otg", "hclk"
- dr_mode: "host"
- vbus-supply: explicit regulator controlling the board's downstream USB power

The Fullhan PMU/clock provider supplies serialized register access. No
second PMU mapping, external PHY or reset phandle is needed for this IP.
Only integrated UTMI 16-bit host is supported. The board describes its power
switch as a regulator; the driver does not assume a GPIO number or voltage.
